Amazon Product Scraper

Turning e-commerce chaos into structured, actionable data.

Overview

In the competitive world of e-commerce, data is king. We built a sophisticated Python-based scraper designed to extract granular product details, real-time pricing, and customer reviews from Amazon at scale. This tool bypasses standard anti-bot protections to deliver reliable, structured data feeds. It empowers businesses to perform competitive analysis, track price fluctuations, and monitor brand sentiment without manual effort.

Key Features

  • Anti-Detection Measures: Utilizes rotating proxies and user-agent switching to mimic human behavior and avoid IP bans.
  • Granular Data Extraction: Captures specific data points including ASIN, BSR, variations, bullet points, and high-res image URLs.
  • Automated Export: Automatically cleans and exports data to CSV, JSON, or directly into a database for immediate analysis.
  • Scalable Workers: Designed with a multi-threaded architecture to scrape thousands of product pages simultaneously.
