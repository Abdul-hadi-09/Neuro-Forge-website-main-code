Instagram Follower Scraper

Actionable social media intelligence at your fingertips.

Overview

Understanding audience demographics and influencer reach is crucial for modern marketing. We developed a specialized Instagram scraper that ethically collects public data from profiles and follower lists. This tool helps marketing agencies and brands identify potential influencers, analyze competitor audiences, and build targeted outreach lists based on bio keywords and engagement metrics.

Key Features

  • Profile Enrichment: Extracts key metrics like follower count, following count, bio text, and public email addresses (if available).
  • Follower Mapping: Capable of iterating through follower lists to build a network graph of related accounts.
  • Rate Limit Management: Intelligent throttling and session management prevent account blocks while maximizing data yield.
  • Export to CRM: Data is formatted perfectly for direct import into marketing tools or CRM software for immediate campaign use.
