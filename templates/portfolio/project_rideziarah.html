RideZiarah Website

Seamless religious tourism booking experience.

Overview

RideZiarah required a modern, fast, and easy-to-use platform for pilgrims to book transportation and tours for Ziarah. We developed a custom web application using Next.js to ensure top-tier performance and SEO rankings. The design focuses on trust and simplicity, allowing users to book rides in just a few clicks. Deployed on Vercel, the site benefits from edge caching and instant scalability during peak pilgrimage seasons.

Key Features

  • Serverless Deployment: Hosted on Vercel for zero-config global scalability and lightning-fast page loads.
  • Dynamic Booking System: Custom form logic that calculates pricing in real-time based on distance, vehicle type, and duration.
  • Localized UX: Designed with cultural nuances in mind to provide a familiar and comfortable booking experience for the target audience.
  • Conversion Optimization: Streamlined user flow with clear calls-to-action resulted in a significant increase in online bookings.
