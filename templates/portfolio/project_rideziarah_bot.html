RideZiarah AI Chatbot

Automating bookings on the world's most popular messaging app.

Overview

To reduce customer support overhead and capture leads 24/7, we engineered a sophisticated AI chatbot for RideZiarah on WhatsApp. Powered by the OpenAI API and Node.js, the bot understands natural language queries, answers FAQs, checks availability, and even facilitates the initial stages of booking. It seamlessly integrates with the business's existing workflow, handing off complex cases to human agents only when necessary.

Key Features

  • Natural Language Processing: Uses GPT capabilities to understand customer intent, even with typos or slang, providing accurate and helpful responses.
  • WhatsApp Business API: Directly integrated into WhatsApp, meeting customers on the platform they already use and love.
  • Automated Booking Flow: Guides users through a structured conversation to gather travel details (dates, pickup, destination) automatically.
  • Instant Notifications: Sends immediate confirmation messages and updates, improving customer trust and reducing no-shows.
