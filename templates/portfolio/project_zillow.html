Zillow Real Estate Scraper

Unlocking the real estate market with high-precision data.

Overview

Real estate investors and agents need up-to-the-minute data to make informed decisions. We built a powerful scraping solution tailored for Zillow, one of the most notoriously difficult sites to scrape. This tool extracts detailed property information, including current listing prices, Zestimate histories, tax data, and agent contact details, transforming raw web pages into clean, analyzable datasets.

Key Features

  • Comprehensive Property Data: Extracts over 50 data points per listing, including school district ratings, HOA fees, and lot size.
  • Historical Price Tracking: Capable of capturing and logging price history to visualize market trends over time.
  • Map-Based Search Support: Can simulate map interactions to scrape listings within specific geographic boundaries or polygons.
  • Stealth Mode: sophisticated browser fingerprinting and delay logic ensure the scraper remains undetected during long extraction runs.
